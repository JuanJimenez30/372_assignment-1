Upcoming Event

The remembrance event for Satoru Gojo, will be held of September 25th 2026 at our local community center.


Don't miss out on this special event!

This event will include various activities and entertainment for all ages which includes:

  • Free admission
  • Meet other local anime fans
  • Food and drinks available for purchase
  • watch party
  • Show off your favorite characters cosplay
  • cosplay competition
  • Share memorable moments with fellow fans

This event is targeted for fans of the Jujutsu Kaisen series. Who would love to spend time with other fans and to remember the legacy of Gojo.

Community Spotlight: Saturo Gojo Remembrance

Event details

Join us and other fellow JJK fans on September 25th in downtown Greensboro. The community gathering is dedicated to honoring the intense fight between Gojo Satoru and Sakuna. The event will feature carefully selected battles and emotional scenes across season 1 and 2 of the Jujutsu Kaisen series. The Event will also be hosting a cosplay competition, where attendees can cosplay and showcase their cosplay's in the competition. The winner will be given a personalized basket filled with Jujutsu Kaisen merchandise and other goodies. The event will also have a variety of japanese cuisines and drinks available for purchase at the event.


Event Activities

  1. Food trucks and stalls for attendees to purchase food and drinks
  2. Meet and greet with other Jujutsu Kaisen fans
  3. Watch selected scenes from the Jujutsu Kaisen series
  4. Registration for cosplayers opens
  5. Cosplay competition for attendees to showcase their cosplay's
  6. Take a community photo at the end of the event

Attendee Tips

  • Arrive early, there will be efficient parking available but the lots may fill up quickly.
  • Bring all your cosplay materials and props to the event.
  • Get in line early for registration to the cosplay competition.
  • Keep personal belongings close to you at all times for safety.

Attendee Testimonial
